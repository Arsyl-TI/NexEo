# NexEo Autonomous Feature Ideas

1. **Video Dual Subtitle Display (Original + Indonesian) (`pages/video/[id].vue`)**
   - Allow playing videos with simultaneous bilingual subtitles (e.g. Japanese/English on top, Indonesian at bottom) for language learners.

2. **Novel Multi-Chapter EPUB Exporter (`pages/novels/[slug]/index.vue`)**
   - Package and export novels into full-fledged `.epub` e-books with covers and table of contents for Apple Books, Kindle, and Kobo e-readers.
