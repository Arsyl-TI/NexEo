# NexEo Autonomous Feature Ideas

1. **Manga / Comic Reader Offline Chapter CBZ/ZIP Exporter (`pages/manga/[slug]/index.vue`)**
   - Allow users to download single chapters or entire manga series packaged as `.cbz` or `.zip` archives for offline comic reading apps (like Tachiyomi / Mihon / Kuro Reader).

2. **Novel Ambient Sound & Background Soundscapes (`pages/novels/[slug]/[chapter].vue`)**
   - Provide built-in relaxing ambient sound generators (e.g. Rain on Window, Campfire, Library Ambience, Cafe) with volume controls while reading novels.
